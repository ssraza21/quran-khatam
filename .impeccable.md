## Design Context

### Users
Muslim individuals, families, community groups, classes, and institutions coordinating shared Qur'an recitation. Participants need to quickly understand a campaign, choose a Surah recitation or Qur'an portion, and see collective progress without administrative complexity getting in the way.

### Brand Personality
Calm, refined, and communal. The interface should feel respectful and reassuring, with a quiet sense of shared purpose and progress rather than urgency or gamification.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light, editorial interface with warm neutrals, deep burgundy brand accents, restrained ornament, and clear typographic hierarchy. Preserve the existing Qur'an Khatam identity while favoring spacious composition, crisp dividers, and purposeful status treatments. Avoid loud gradients, playful visual language, excessive card nesting, and attention-seeking motion.

### Design Principles
- Make campaign structure immediately legible: Surah recitations and full Qur'an Khatams are distinct paths.
- Keep participation actions clear and welcoming for users with varying technical confidence.
- Use color, typography, and motion sparingly to communicate state—not merely to decorate.
- Preserve multiline devotional and campaign text exactly as organizers compose it.
- Design mobile-first and maintain readable contrast, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support.
